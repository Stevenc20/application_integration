## Slide 3 – PPC Schedule: Master Data

4 tabel master data:

**line_masters** – Definisi line produksi: line_code (unique), line_name, shift (Pagi/Malam), capacity, machine_count, status (active/inactive/maintenance)

**job_masters** – Tabel sentral setiap job: job_number (unique), job_name, target_qty, status, plan_start, plan_end. Penghubung utama ke hampir semua tabel lainnya.

**job_processes** – Proses kerja per job: job_master_id FK (cascade), process_name, sequence_no, standard_minutes

**master_stampings** – Data referensi stamping: job_no, part_no, part_name, ct_detik, dct, mct, tpt, customer

## Slide 4 – PPC Schedule: Penjadwalan

5 tabel penjadwalan inti:

**production_plans** – Tabel TERBESAR dengan 60+ kolom. Mencakup: line_master_id FK, plan_date, shift_name, press_name, row_type (job/break), job_no, plan, ok, repair, reject, ct_detik, dct, mct, dt_menit, p1-p4 (status mesin), start_time, finish_time, act_start, act_finish, source_type (ppc/recovery)

**schedule_stampings** – Upload Excel jadwal stamping: upload_date, press_name, shift_name, job_no

**recovery_schedules** – Recovery dengan approval workflow: status (waiting_approval/approved/rejected/scheduled)

**recovery_items** – Item recovery: recovery_schedule_id FK, production_plan_id FK, plan_qty, ok, repair, reject

**schedule_revisions** – Audit trail: snapshot_before & snapshot_after (JSON)

## Slide 5 – Breaktime

2 tabel pengaturan jam istirahat:

**master_break_times** – Master jadwal istirahat: hari, shift, waktu_mulai (time), waktu_selesai (time), type (istirahat/cinkorak), is_active

**break_times** – Versi lama: nama_istirahat, shift, hari, waktu_mulai, waktu_selesai

Terhubung secara logis ke production_plans melalui pencocokan hari + shift

## Slide 6 – Production Flow

4 tabel tracking eksekusi produksi real-time:

**production_sessions** – Sesi produksi: job_master_id FK, work_date, start_time, pause_time, finish_time, status (idle → running → paused → finished)

**daily_productions** – Rekap harian: job_master_id FK, work_date, line, shift, target_qty, actual_ok, actual_repair, actual_reject, efficiency

**production_logs** – Log per event: job_master_id, ok_qty, repair_qty, reject_qty

**production_targets** – Target produksi: target_date, job_id FK, process_type, shift, target_qty

## Slide 10 – Key Performance Indicators

8 metrik utama yang didukung data real:

| KPI | Rumus | Sumber Data |
|-----|-------|-------------|
| Plan vs Actual | plan − actual_ok | production_plans + daily_productions |
| Efficiency | (actual_ok ÷ target_qty) × 100% | daily_productions |
| Repair Rate | actual_repair ÷ total produksi | daily_productions |
| Reject Rate | actual_reject ÷ total produksi | daily_productions |
| Downtime | Total per jenis_downtime | downtimes |
| Changeover Time | Rata-rata duration_minutes | dandoris |
| Recovery | plan_qty vs actual_qty | recovery_items |
| OEE | Availability × Performance × Quality | Gabungan seluruh domain |

## Slide 12 – Flowchart Alur Produksi Keseluruhan

{masukan gambar flowchart alur lengkap dari hulu ke hilir}

**Alur lengkap:**
1. **Perencanaan (PPC):** Upload jadwal → Validasi master data → Jika target tidak tercapai → Recovery (approval)
2. **Eksekusi (Operator):** Start sesi → Produksi → Catat hasil → End shift → Rekap harian + Efficiency
3. **Handling Masalah:** Mesin rusak → Machine log | Hambatan → Downtime | Defect → Repair/reject log | Ganti job → Dandori
4. **Evaluasi (Manager):** 8 KPI utama + 8 laporan manajerial + Audit trail JSON
